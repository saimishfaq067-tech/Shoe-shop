# 👟 ShoeShop Pro POS

A professional and customizable **Shoe Shop Point of Sale (POS) & Inventory Management System** built with Python and Streamlit.

## 🚀 Features

### 🛒 Point of Sale
- Fast product search
- Product name search
- Brand search
- SKU search
- Barcode search
- Size and color search
- Voice product search 🎙️
- Add products to cart
- Quantity management
- Discount
- Tax calculation
- Multiple payment methods
- Automatic stock deduction
- Invoice generation

### 👟 Inventory Management
- Product management
- Brand management
- Category
- Size-wise inventory
- Color management
- SKU
- Barcode
- Purchase price
- Sale price
- Stock quantity
- Low-stock monitoring
- Bulk product import
- CSV import
- Excel import
- Add 100 demo products with one click

### 👥 Customer Management
- Customer profiles
- Phone number
- Address
- Credit / Udhaar tracking
- Customer records

### 💰 Expense Management
- Rent
- Electricity
- Salaries
- Transport
- Marketing
- Maintenance
- Other expenses

### 📊 Reports
- Total sales
- Total cost
- Gross profit
- Number of invoices
- Payment method reports
- Sold products
- Excel report export

### 🎙️ Voice Search

The POS includes voice-based product search.

For example, the seller can say:

> Nike

or:

> Nike Running

The system searches matching products by:

- Product name
- Brand
- Category
- Size
- Color
- SKU
- Barcode

Both English and Urdu voice recognition are supported.
